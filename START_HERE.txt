Your wedding gallery is ready! Just 2 simple steps:


┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 1: CONVERT PHOTOS (First time only - ~3 minutes)        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Open Terminal in this folder and run:

    ./convert_heic.sh

This converts 440 iPhone photos (HEIC) to web-compatible JPEG.
You'll see progress updates every 50 files.


┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 2: START THE GALLERY                                     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

After conversion completes, run:

    ./start_gallery.sh

Your browser will open automatically with the gallery!
